HOW THE APP DECIDES (so the statuses make sense)
  Writing screens (vsay): a line is a list of pieces, e.g. "Let's write a capital" + "A". If EVERY piece has a clip, her clips play.
    If even one piece is missing, the WHOLE line is said by the device voice instead (so one missing word un-records the line).
  My Farm (fRespond / fAmbient): only pieces with a clip play; a piece without a clip is skipped, silently. Never the device voice.
    My Farm matches clips ignoring capitals and extra spaces (exact text first); the writing screens need the exact text.
  Animal sounds and stamps (dClip): her clip, or nothing. In the Barnyard an unrecorded animal sound becomes a made-up beep sound.
  "You wrote your name!" is the only line that goes straight to the device voice (speak), after her clips.
  Punctuation always has to match.
  If a writing-screen clip cannot be loaded (e.g. offline before it was cached), that line falls back to the device voice.

WHO HEARS IT: every spoken line below is heard by the child in normal play. Grown-ups, the first-run screen ("Who is learning
today?"), Who's playing?, progress reports, progress files and Class view never speak.
